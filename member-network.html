Member Network

A growing network of golfers, entrepreneurs, professionals, and business owners connected through golf.

What the Network Is For

A practical member platform for connection and visibility.

Member Introductions

Helping members understand who is in the room, what they do, and where relevant connections may exist.

Business Visibility

Creating appropriate opportunities for members to share their businesses, services, and professional experience.

Relationship Building

Using golf days and club gatherings to build trust over repeated interaction.

Knowledge Sharing

Encouraging members to exchange practical lessons, insights, and guidance in an informal club setting.

Member Directory Coming Soon

Birdie Squad is preparing a member network experience that can showcase participating members and businesses in a responsible, permission-based way.

Until the directory is live, members can use club events, golf days, and contact channels to introduce themselves and build relationships.

  • Member profiles by permission
  • Business categories and services
  • Event-based introductions
  • Professional and entrepreneurial visibility

Who It Serves

The network is built around useful, respectful connection.

Golfers

Members who enjoy the game and want a club environment that goes beyond the scorecard.

Entrepreneurs

Business builders looking for trusted relationships and practical visibility.

Professionals

People who value a credible network for conversation, referrals, and collaboration.
