Club Activities

Golf, networking, visibility, and knowledge sharing for members who want to connect and grow.

Club Golf

Golf Days & Club Rounds

Birdie Squad creates regular opportunities for members to enjoy golf in a relaxed, professional, and relationship-driven setting.

Club rounds and golf days are designed to help members spend quality time together, build trust, and create conversations that extend beyond the course.

What Members Can Expect

  • Recreational rounds and social golf days
  • Welcoming environments for different playing levels
  • Time for introductions before and after play
  • A club culture that values respect and enjoyment
Relationship Building

Business Networking Through Golf

Golf gives members time and space to connect in ways that traditional networking rooms often cannot.

The club supports natural introductions between entrepreneurs, professionals, business owners, and golfers who want to grow their circle through trusted relationships.

Networking Focus Areas

  • Member introductions
  • Business conversations around club events
  • Referral-friendly relationship building
  • Connections between established and emerging professionals
Member Platform

Member Business Visibility

Birdie Squad gives members opportunities to share who they are, what they do, and how their businesses or professional services can add value.

This may include introductions, event visibility, member spotlights, and business showcase opportunities where appropriate.

Visibility Opportunities

  • Member business introductions
  • Event-based showcase opportunities
  • Peer recommendations and referrals
  • Professional profile building within the club network
Shared Growth

Mentorship and Knowledge Sharing

The club encourages members to share practical knowledge, lessons, and experience in a respectful and informal way.

Mentorship and guidance can happen through conversation, introductions, peer support, and knowledge-sharing moments around club activities.

Examples

  • Business lessons from experienced members
  • Guidance for emerging entrepreneurs
  • Peer-to-peer advice and accountability
  • Relationship-led support between members

Positive Community Presence

A club can build value through responsible relationships and good conduct.

Birdie Squad aims to be a constructive presence in the golf and business community. The club may support positive initiatives, create opportunities for connection, and encourage members to contribute where they can, while keeping its public communication focused on recreational golf, networking, and member growth.

Join a Club Activity

Whether you want to play, connect, showcase your business, or share knowledge, Birdie Squad has a place for you.
