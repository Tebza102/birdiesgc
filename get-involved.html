Who Should Join?

Birdie Squad is for people who enjoy golf and understand that relationships are built through time, trust, and shared experiences.

The club is especially relevant for golfers, entrepreneurs, professionals, business owners, and leaders who want to connect with others in a relaxed but purposeful environment.

  • Golfers looking for a relationship-led club environment
  • Entrepreneurs and business owners seeking credible visibility
  • Professionals interested in practical networking
  • Members who value respect, inclusion, and growth
