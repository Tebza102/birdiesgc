About Birdie Squad

A recreational golf and networking club where golf creates relationships, business connection, and positive growth.

Our Mission

Birdie Squad Golf Club exists to bring golfers, entrepreneurs, professionals, and business owners together through recreational golf and meaningful relationships.

We believe golf is more than a round on the course. It is a natural setting for conversation, trust, visibility, and long-term personal and business growth.

Our Vision

To become a respected golf and networking club known for inclusive membership, professional relationships, business visibility, and a positive club culture.

Our Brand Pillars

The foundation of how the club operates.

Recreation

Golf remains at the centre of the club experience, creating relaxed and enjoyable ways for members to connect.

Relationships

The club values genuine introductions, repeat interaction, and relationships that grow over time.

Business Growth

Members can build visibility, exchange ideas, and discover opportunities within a trusted network.

Inclusion

Birdie Squad welcomes golfers, entrepreneurs, professionals, and business owners who share the club's values.

Integrity

We aim to operate responsibly, communicate honestly, and avoid claims that are not supported by verified records.

What Sets Us Apart

A recreational club with social awareness.

A Recreational Club With Social Awareness

Birdie Squad is not positioned as a charity or tax-benefit organisation. The club focuses on golf, networking, member visibility, and positive relationships while remaining mindful of the communities and businesses around it.

A Practical Business Network

Golf creates a trusted environment for business owners and professionals to meet, talk, and understand each other beyond a formal boardroom setting.

Professional Club Principles

The club aims to be clear, responsible, and transparent about what it does, how members participate, and how partners can support club activities.

Be Part of the Club

Join Birdie Squad if you value golf, relationships, business visibility, and positive growth.
