Governance & Club Principles

Responsible administration, clear communication, and a professional club culture.

How We Position the Club

Clear, compliance-aware language about what Birdie Squad is.

Recreational Club

Birdie Squad is positioned as a recreational golf and networking club.

Inclusive Membership

The club welcomes members who value golf, relationships, business visibility, and positive conduct.

Responsible Administration

The club aims to manage activities, partnerships, and communication with care and consistency.

Compliance Awareness

We avoid unverified legal, tax, impact, or endorsement claims in public communication.

Club Leadership

Guided by members and committee processes.

Committee-Guided Administration

The club is guided by its committee and members in line with its constitution and internal governance processes.

Verified leadership details can be published when approved by the club.

Operating Principles

The standards that guide club activity.

Member Respect

Members are expected to engage respectfully on the course, at events, and in business conversations connected to the club.

Clear Communication

Club activities and partnership opportunities should be described accurately and without unsupported claims.

Data Protection

The club aims to handle member and inquiry information responsibly and in line with applicable privacy expectations.

Partner Alignment

Partnerships should support club activities, member value, and positive relationship building.

Responsible Club Growth

Birdie Squad is building a credible recreational golf and networking club through clear positioning, member participation, and careful communication.

Contact the Club

Golf. Networking. Growth.

Birdie Squad Golf Club is a recreational golf and networking club based in Ekurhuleni (NPO 273-353), connecting golfers, entrepreneurs, professionals, and business owners through golf, relationships, and positive growth.
